# Office Focus High Stress Pack

Use this skill when:

- `location_state` is `office`
- `stress_score >= 60`
- DND and write-settings permissions are granted

Actions:

- Enable DND.
- Set ringer to vibrate.
- Set brightness target to 40 percent.
- Schedule feedback prompt after 30 minutes.

Safety:

- Do not apply during emergency contacts mode.
- Do not reapply within a 45 minute cooldown.
